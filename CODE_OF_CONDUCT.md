# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want the TaskTide use-case registry to be a welcoming, constructive, inclusive, and respectful community.

Contributors, reviewers, maintainers, and users are expected to participate in good faith and to treat one another with respect.

---

## 1). Expected Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Giving constructive feedback.
* Discussing ideas and technical decisions openly and in good faith.
* Focusing criticism on ideas, implementations, and contributions rather than individuals.
* Respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, perspectives, and approaches.
* Taking responsibility for mistakes and working constructively to resolve disagreements.

---

## 2). Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
* Sexualised or otherwise inappropriate conduct.
* Publishing someone's private or personal information without permission.
* Threats or abusive behaviour.
* Deliberately derailing technical discussions or community processes.
* Any other behaviour that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

---

## 3). Pull Requests and Technical Discussion

Technical disagreement is expected and encouraged.

Reviewers may question an implementation, request changes, or reject a contribution. Contributors may disagree with review feedback.

These discussions should remain focused on the technical merits of the contribution and should be conducted respectfully.

Passing automated evaluation does not guarantee that a contribution will be accepted. Maintainers may request changes where appropriate.

---

## 4). Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the repository maintainers.

Please do not use public Issues or Pull Requests to report sensitive incidents.

Maintainers are responsible for addressing behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

* Ask participants to modify or stop inappropriate behaviour.
* Remove inappropriate comments or content.
* Close discussions or Pull Requests.
* Temporarily or permanently restrict participation in the repository.

Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from the community.

---

## 6). Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within this repository and to other spaces where someone is representing the TaskTide use-case community.

---

This Code of Conduct is intended to support a respectful and constructive community. It does not replace the repository's contribution guidelines or technical review process.
